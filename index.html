about

Sophomore undergraduate at NYU.

My current research focuses on implementing Simulation-Based Inference techniques for the CAMELS simulation framework to constrain astrophysical and cosmological parameters.

Previously, I identified and published what is currently the largest sample of high-redshift supermassive black holes (BLAGN) from JWST/NIRSpec.

Before that, I worked on synapse pruning and the aging hippocampus at Stanford.
